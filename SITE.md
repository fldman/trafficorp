# TraffiCorp

> We don't go with the flow. We control it.

Traffic control for road work across Idaho and Washington: flaggers, pilot cars, signals, message boards, full work-zone setups and the plans behind them.

## About This Site

**Primary goal:** Get quote requests from people who need a work zone staffed, often by tomorrow. Second goal: recruit crew members (on the Careers page, kept off the buyer homepage).
**Who it's for:** Construction and road maintenance contractors, utility companies, state and federal project managers, and people looking for flagging jobs.
**The problem we solve:** A work zone has to be safe, compliant and staffed on time. TraffiCorp runs the whole thing (people, equipment, tech and the traffic control plan) from one call to dispatch.

## Brand Identity

- **Personality:** Industrial, precise and calm. Engineering-drawing details instead of decoration. It looks like the work: signs, plan sheets, message boards.
- **Colours:**
  - **TraffiCorp amber `#F79521`** (from the logo) is the brand colour. It's used like a road sign: as a *background with black text on it*, never as text on white (it's too light to read that way).
  - Black ink `#151515` for text, grey `#4B4B4B` for body copy, `#6B6B6B` for small labels
  - Asphalt `#111112` and night `#040608` for dark sections. On dark backgrounds amber *can* be used for text.
  - Concrete `#EAEEF0` and paper `#F5F5F5` for light sections and cards
- **Fonts:** Inter Tight for everything; JetBrains Mono for small "plan sheet" labels, numbers, the work order and tickets.
- **Signature details:**
  - **The sign mark:** the key phrase in a heading sits on an amber band, like the legend on a work-zone sign (for example "We control it.", "entire work zone", "last cone.").
  - **Sign buttons:** an amber label panel with a black arrow plate next to it.
  - **Warning-lamp labels:** each section's small label ("HOW IT WORKS", "COVERAGE"…) has an amber lamp beside it, like the lights on road barricades. Every time it scrolls into view it pulses twice, slowly, then stays lit. (It doesn't blink non-stop on purpose: accessibility rules limit blinking to five seconds.)
  - **The Line:** an amber road line that runs through the site. It merges through cones, becomes the centre line of a lane-shift drawing, traces the corridor on the coverage map, and stops by tracing the quote card at the bottom.

## Accessibility

- Text meets WCAG AA contrast, including text over photos (checked by sampling the actual pixels). Amber is only ever a background for black text on light pages.
- Small text has a minimum size and grows when you zoom the browser.
- Works with a keyboard: skip link, visible focus rings, a focus-trapped menu and quote form (Escape closes them), tech tiles and form chips you can reach with Tab, and a town search on the coverage map that works with the arrow keys and Enter (and tells screen readers the answer).
- Screen readers get real headings, landmarks, labelled fields, error messages tied to their fields, and the final figures in the counters.
- Moving things can be paused (the message board stops while hovered or focused and has a pause button for keyboard users), blinking lamps and the scroll hint stop within five seconds, and "reduce motion" turns off the loading screen, smooth scrolling and scroll animations, with every drawing shown complete.
- Automated audit (axe-core, WCAG 2.2 AA plus best practice): no violations on any page.

## How to Customize

- **Change homepage wording:** `content/home.ts`. About: `content/about.ts`. Careers: `content/careers.ts`. Contact: `content/contact.ts`.
- **Change the phone number, email, offices, hours or the Apply link:** `lib/site.ts`. They update everywhere at once.
- **Change the founding year:** `founded` in `lib/site.ts`.
- **Change which phrase gets the amber sign mark:** the `mark` field next to a title in the content files.
- **Switch Smart tech back to the grid:** in `app/page.tsx`, add `layout="bento"` to the `Tech` line.
- **Switch the "Our answer" opening:** in `app/page.tsx`, add `intro="taper"` (lane lines and cones) or `intro="paddle"` (one large turning paddle) to the `Solution` line.
- **Swap a photo:** put the new picture in `public/images/photos/` with the same name, or change the file name in the content files.
- **Change colours:** the colour list at the top of `app/globals.css`.
- **Make the forms actually send:** `app/actions/quote.ts` and `app/actions/contact.ts`.
- **Add a page:** create a folder in `app/` with a `page.tsx`, and build it from the sections in `components/sections/`.
- **Put the site on Google later:** remove the "robots" lines in `app/layout.tsx`.
